accessibility

this site should work for everyone. here's what it does, and how to tell me when it doesn't.

what's built in

  • Every page works with only a keyboard. Press Tab to move, and the first stop is a "skip to content" link.
  • Pages use real headings, landmarks and labels, so screen readers like VoiceOver, NVDA and TalkBack can navigate them.
  • Images have text descriptions. Enlarged images open in a dialog you can close with Esc.
  • Text colors meet WCAG AA contrast, and there's a high-contrast mode.
  • The Aa button (top right) lets you enlarge text, switch on high contrast, turn off motion, or have the page read aloud.
  • The site follows your device's reduced-motion setting automatically.
  • There is no audio or video that plays on its own. Anything with sound will come with captions or a transcript.

something not working?

Message me on LinkedIn and tell me what you were trying to do. I'll fix it.
